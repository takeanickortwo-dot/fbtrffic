⚡ The 30-Minute Rule
You post. You sit there for 30 minutes and reply to EVERY comment with a follow-up question. Not "thanks" — a question. This signals Facebook to push your stuff into more feeds. Example replies: • "What year was that?" • "Where exactly was that located?" • "Do you think they'd try that here?" • "Who else remembers this?"
🔗 Link Strategy
Facebook buries posts with outbound links in the main text. DO: Write your post. Put "Link in comments 👇" at the end. Paste the Substack URL as the FIRST comment immediately after posting. DON'T: Paste links in the main post body. Facebook's algorithm treats it as an exit signal and throttles reach.
📹 Go Live Once a Week
Even 5 minutes. Walk downtown Staunton. Point at a building and tell its story. Or rant about a Dominion bill. Facebook pushes Lives harder than any other format. Pro tip: Announce it 24 hours ahead in the group. "Going live tomorrow at 7 PM to talk about [topic]."
